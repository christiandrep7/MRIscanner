🧠 MRIscanner

Brain tumor classification demo (glioma / meningioma / pituitary / no tumor), running a EfficientNet-B0 model in your browser session via a serverless ONNX Runtime function. Not a diagnostic tool.

Click or drop an MRI scan here
Model: EfficientNet-B0, trained on the Kaggle Brain Tumor MRI Dataset. Held-out test accuracy: 92.7%.
Source: github.com/christiandrep7/MRIscanner
